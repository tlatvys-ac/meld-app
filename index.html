MELD

Push Figma boards into After Effects as live, editable layers

Download Meld AE panel + Figma plugin (.zip) · free

Made for macOS. The AE panel's installer may show a certificate notice. What to expect

About

Meld turns a Figma board into an After Effects comp that matches it as closely as After Effects allows. Shapes, text, gradients, masks and groups arrive as real, editable layers you can animate right away. Whatever After Effects can't rebuild comes over as a crisp bitmap, and Meld tells you exactly which layers those are and why.

It's two small pieces that talk on your own machine: a Figma plugin with four buttons, and a panel in After Effects that receives what you send.

What it looks like

Features

  • Live, editable layersShapes, text, gradients, masks, nulls and precomps, built the way you'd build them by hand
  • Text stays textFonts, size, tracking, leading, caps and mixed styles, lined up on Figma's own glyphs
  • Bitmaps only where neededImage fills, angular gradients, inner shadows and the rest arrive as crisp 1–4× bitmaps
  • Nothing changed silentlyEvery approximation is a named note, and a hidden Figma Reference layer lets you compare
  • Three push modesPush Selection, Split Layers (one layer per shape piece) or Single Layer (shapes combined)
  • Rasterize into the active compExactly where the layer sits in its frame, and whole even where the board crops it
  • Comps and frames, both waysMake a comp from a Figma frame, or a Figma frame from the active comp
  • Straight back to After EffectsAfter Effects comes to the front as soon as a push lands

Setup

  1. Download and unzip Meld. Keep the Meld Figma Plugin folder somewhere permanent: Figma loads the plugin from it.
  2. Install the After Effects panel. Open Meld_v0.2.0.zxp with a ZXP installer (aescripts ZXP Installer is free).
  3. Open the panel. Restart After Effects and choose Window → Extensions → Meld. Leave it open while you work: it's what receives your pushes.
  4. Add the Figma plugin. In the Figma desktop app, choose Plugins → Development → Import plugin from manifest… and pick manifest.json in the Meld Figma Plugin folder.
  5. Push. Select a frame, run Plugins → Development → Meld, and press Push. The board arrives as a new comp and After Effects comes to the front.

If the installer warns you

The After Effects panel is signed with a self-signed certificate, so your ZXP installer may show an "unverified publisher" notice. That's expected; go ahead and install.

Nothing else needs approving: the Figma plugin is a folder of plain files, and Figma lists it under Development because it's installed from your Mac instead of the Figma Community.

Where things go

Bitmaps are saved in a Meld Assets folder next to your After Effects project (or in Documents for an unsaved project), and everything Meld builds goes in a Meld folder in the Project panel.

Uninstalling

Remove the panel with your ZXP installer, or delete the Meld folder it installed, in one of these:

~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/Meld
/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/Meld

In Figma, open Plugins → Manage plugins and remove Meld from Development.

Requirements

macOS · After Effects 2023 or later (developed and tested on After Effects 2026 and 2027) · the Figma desktop app (Figma runs development plugins only there). Everything stays on your Mac: the plugin talks only to the Meld panel, on your own machine. No accounts, no uploads.
